## What happened
- Read every `ai_memory` file, the script and the installed settings. The owner uses the Graphs style. The only "you are here" sign was a faint tile background and a 16×3 bar, so both tiles looked almost the same. The owner's taskbar has a reddish tint from the wallpaper, so designs were checked on that color too.
- Built D22 (see `02_decisions`): each PC gets a color in `$Sources` (Nvidia `#76B900`, AMD `#ED1C24` at first); this PC's tile is tinted with a solid name tag (`NameTag`) in every style; a big "You're on AMD" card (`NameBanner`) shows after the monitor switches to this PC, on the first mouse or keyboard input after 1.5 s; the tray icon is filled with the PC's color and its tooltip names it; the panel gets a "This PC" chip; a tray item turns the card off (`NameOnSwitch`). The purple on-screen bar no longer shows under this PC's tile. The input poll also runs while the card is on, even with the widget hidden.
- Checked the look with offscreen renders (all four styles × reddish / dark / light taskbars × both PCs, the panel tiles, the card, the tray icons) without showing anything on screen. Made the card about 25% bigger after the first render, since the screen is a 49-inch ultrawide.
- An AI-tool gotcha again: the file editor turned three typed `\u2019` / `\u201C` escapes into real characters. The ASCII check caught them and Python put the escapes back (`chr(92)`).
- A logic test drove `App.InputChanged` with the real code: 17 checks passed (no card at startup or when leaving; input in the first 1.5 s ignored; shows on the next input; click-through; no focus change; centered; gone after about 2.5 s; cancelled by leaving again or the menu switch). It showed the card on the AMD PC's desktop once.
- Installed on the AMD PC by building from the checkout into the install folder (setup's own fill-in and manifest, no panel). The taskbar shows the red AMD tag. The monitor was on the Nvidia PC at that moment (the AMD widget correctly put the on-screen bar under Nvidia).
- A script watching for the card's window saw it show for 2.6 s at 23:32 when the owner switched back to the AMD PC: it works for real.
- The owner said "the red is too red, make it maroon or deeper darker slightly". Rendered six shades from `#ED1C24` to `#800000` on the taskbar's colors; `#7D1420` and `#800000` blended into the reddish taskbar, `#B8141E` was barely different. Picked `#8E1622` (maroon) and installed it on the AMD PC. Git Bash's `sed -i` turned the script's CRLF line endings into LF on the way; Python put CRLF back. Use Python or the Edit tool on the .ps1, not `sed -i`.
- Updated the README and these notes. The owner said "Looks good, commit and push it": committed and pushed to `main` (`6112bdd`).
- The owner said "remove the you are on amd or nvidia pop up i dont want that". Took the card out completely and pushed it (`497ca04`).
